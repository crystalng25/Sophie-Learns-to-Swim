Sophie and her mother live on an island  far out in the ocean. Mother wants Sophie to learn how to swim. But Sophie is scared of being in water.  The ocean is sooooo large and deep!
Today, the waves are small.  So Mother takes Sophie to the lagoon. “Sophie, please get in! The lagoon is shallow,” says Mother. “Can I learn to swim next time, Mother? Pleeeeeease!”
“Don’t be scared, Sophie.  See how all the animals swim.  Try moving your legs and arms just like them.” Sophie peers into the water.  So many colourful animals are  all around her! Thooooo! The water is very salty.
Sophie takes a deep breath and dips her head in the water. She watches the animals swim, just as Mother had told her to.
A baby turtle swims by, kicking her legs and pushing the  water aside with her flippers. Sophie tries to swim like the turtle.  She accidentally gulps some water. “Mother, the water stings!” cries Sophie. “It will get easier,” says Mother.
The next day, a baby octopus jets by. He draws water in and pushes his eight arms forward. Sophie tries to swim just like  the octopus.
She swallows some water. Ugghh! But she tries again.
“Watch me kick my legs, Mother!” yells Sophie. “Good! Keep trying.”
The next day an eagle ray soars by,  flapping her triangular wings. Sophie tries to swim like the ray. She swallows some water, and sinks a little — blub-blub-blub. “This is so difficult, Mother!” says Sophie. “Keep trying, Sophie!” Sophie tries again. And again.
The next day, some plankton float by. They have no flippers or wings. They move with the waves. Sophie stretches her arms and legs. She floats like the plankton.
“Mother, watch me float!” shouts Sophie. “That’s great, Sophie!”
The next day, an eel wiggles by.  He swings from side to side, like a snake. Sophie tries to swim like the eel. “Sophie, you are swimming!” says Mother.
The next day, a flying fish leaps by.  She glides through the air. Sophie tries to swim like the flying fish. She flails and crashes into the water. “My arms and legs hurt, Mother!”  says Sophie. “Once you remember to breathe while swimming, your arms and legs will move slower. Then it will hurt less.”
The next day, a dolphin zips by. He comes to the surface of the water, breathes deeply, and then dives. Sophie tries to swim like the dolphin. She lifts her head out of water, gulps some air, and dives back in.
As the days go by, Sophie watches more and more animals swim. She learns many new moves. Sophie kicks. She floats. She wiggles. She dives. She holds her breath for longer and longer. She swims faster and faster.
“Sophie, it's time to go!” yells Mother. “Can I swim just a little bit longer? Pleeease, Mother?”
Squid and octopuses draw water in and expel it very fast.
Tiny plankton drift wherever the water takes them.
Have You Tried Swimming? Different animals have different ways of moving through water.
Tuna and dolphins are shaped to shoot through the water with great speed.
Flying fish leap out of water to escape predators.
Try some of these moves the next time you step into the water!
